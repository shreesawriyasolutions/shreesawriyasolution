About Our Training

Practical Knowledge. Real Repair Skills.

At Shree Sawriya Solutions, our training is focused on developing practical mobile repair and motherboard troubleshooting skills.

The training combines electronics fundamentals with hands-on practical work so that students can understand:

  • How a mobile motherboard works
  • How to identify components
  • How to test components
  • How to find faults
  • How to read schematics
  • How to use boardview
  • How to diagnose short circuits
  • How to perform microsoldering
  • How to approach chip-level repairs

The objective is to help students understand the diagnosis process, rather than simply replacing parts without identifying the actual fault.
